# Mirae — Post-MVP Product Vision

> Canonical product direction for the next Mirae development cycle.

## 1. Product thesis

Mirae is not only a commission-management dashboard.

Mirae should become the complete public and private operating system for independent digital artists:

- a beautiful public artist studio;
- a portfolio;
- an artist-specific link-in-bio hub;
- a structured commission request flow;
- a private workspace for requests, quotes, queue, clients, revisions and deliveries;
- a client portal for progress, feedback and files.

The product should replace the fragmented stack many artists currently maintain:

- Linktree or another link-in-bio page;
- Carrd or a small portfolio site;
- Google Forms or Typeform;
- Trello, Notion or spreadsheets;
- email and direct messages for follow-up;
- WeTransfer or Drive for delivery.

Mirae should connect all these moments into one coherent journey.

## 2. Core positioning

### Public promise

> One beautiful home for your art, links and commissions.

Alternative copy:

> Your art, links and commissions — all in one studio.

### Private promise

> Your private commission studio.

### Product split

The public studio is for:

- identity;
- portfolio;
- featured links;
- social presence;
- commission availability;
- commission types;
- commission intake.

The private workspace is for:

- requests;
- clients;
- commission queue;
- quotes;
- payment tracking;
- revisions;
- delivery;
- activity history.

## 3. What Mirae is not

Mirae is not:

- a marketplace;
- a discovery feed;
- an escrow service;
- a social network;
- a generic website builder;
- a generic Linktree clone;
- a platform taking a percentage of commission revenue.

Mirae remains subscription-first and workflow-first.

## 4. Strategic differentiation

Mirae combines four systems that are usually disconnected:

1. **Artist identity** — portfolio, links, social presence.
2. **Commission intake** — commission types, availability, structured forms.
3. **Commission operations** — queue, clients, quote, status, delivery.
4. **Client experience** — portal, feedback, revisions and files.

The differentiation is not merely “more features”.

It is the continuity between these systems.

A visitor discovers an artist, sees their work, checks availability, sends a request, becomes a client, follows progress and receives files without leaving the same ecosystem.

## 5. Experience principles

### Public studio principles

- The artwork comes first.
- The artist identity is immediate but not visually dominant.
- Commission availability is understandable in seconds.
- Links are curated, not a stack of identical buttons.
- The request flow feels like part of the studio.
- The page should be credible enough to become the artist’s main public link.

### Private workspace principles

- Calm and operational.
- Dense enough to be useful, never visually noisy.
- Fast status changes.
- Clear ownership of every request, client and delivery.
- Important actions remain accessible on mobile.
- No dead buttons or fake controls.

### Customization principles

Mirae should offer controlled expression, not arbitrary page building.

Artists may customize:

- accent palette;
- typography preset;
- hero layout;
- portfolio density;
- section order;
- section visibility;
- image radius;
- featured content.

Artists should not initially receive:

- arbitrary custom CSS;
- arbitrary fonts;
- scripts;
- raw HTML;
- unrestricted color combinations;
- layouts that break accessibility.

## 6. Target users

Primary users:

- character illustrators;
- VTuber artists;
- emote artists;
- concept artists;
- OC commission artists;
- digital painters;
- independent artists who already attract clients through social media.

Secondary users:

- small artist teams;
- assistants or managers;
- artists who occasionally open limited commission slots.

## 7. Core success loop

The ideal Mirae loop is:

1. Artist creates a studio.
2. Artist uploads portfolio work.
3. Artist adds links and commission types.
4. Artist publishes and shares one URL.
5. Visitor discovers the artist’s work.
6. Visitor starts a commission request.
7. Artist accepts and converts it into a commission.
8. Artist sends a quote.
9. Client follows progress through the portal.
10. Artist delivers files.
11. Client leaves feedback.
12. Artist reuses Mirae for the next commission.

## 8. Activation definition

An artist is considered activated when they have:

- completed their profile;
- added at least one commission type;
- uploaded at least three portfolio images;
- published their public studio;
- copied or shared the studio link.

A stronger activation event is receiving the first real request.

## 9. North-star direction

Mirae should become:

> The default public link and private workflow for independent commission artists.

The roadmap should prioritize the public studio, mobile quality, onboarding, trust and real artist testing before advanced billing or enterprise features.
