# Beta recruiting — outreach messages (BETA-002)

Copy-paste messages to approach digital artists for the closed beta. Fill the
`[brackets]`. Rules that keep these from reading as spam:

- **Lead with their work** — a specific, genuine compliment (name a piece/series).
  Never send a version where `[compliment]` is generic.
- **Be a person, not a launch.** Short, human, no marketing gloss.
- **Be upfront**: free, private beta, you want feedback, it's early.
- **One ask**: a 30-min setup + a short chat. No pressure.
- **Never overclaim.** It's a workspace to manage commissions — not clients/sales.
- Personalize the platform (DM vs email) and don't mass-paste identically.

---
